GrinLoft Pinner

GrinLoft Pinner is a small in-house tool used only by the GrinLoft team. It helps us share our own products on our own Pinterest business account, pinterest.com/grinloftshop. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

  • Creates Pins for GrinLoft's own Etsy listings on boards owned by the GrinLoft account. Each Pin's image, title, description and link come from our own listing.
  • A person approves every Pin. A GrinLoft team member reviews and approves each Pin one by one before it is published. There is no bulk or unattended posting.
  • Reads our own boards and analytics (impressions, saves, outbound clicks on GrinLoft Pins) so we can see which products people find useful.
  • Uses Pinterest Trends to choose relevant keywords for our own Pin descriptions.

The app only connects to the GrinLoft Pinterest account. It does not access other users' data, read messages, follow, comment or save other people's Pins. Every Pin links to the matching product page in our Etsy shop.
